How it works

Open the Olesia bot on Telegram

One tap — no account, no email.

Add what to watch

Paste an address or xpub (watch-only), a price level, or a fee target.

Get pinged

The bot messages you the moment it happens. Mute or edit any alert anytime.

✈️ Open the Olesia bot on Telegram Free while in beta · open source — see the repo.
Non-custodial by design. Olesia only ever watches public data — addresses and xpubs reveal balances and history but cannot spend. You never send a seed or a private key to get alerts, and you shouldn't: no legitimate notification service needs one.
