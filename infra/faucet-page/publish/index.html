Olesia.

The earlier Olesia wallet that lived at this address, including its testnet modes, has been retired.

The Olesia wallet is now at olesia.io.

Had a wallet here? Your bitcoin is not stored on this site — it is on the blockchain, controlled by your recovery words. At olesia.io choose “Import a wallet” and enter your 12 or 24 words (and your passphrase, if you set one). Your balance will appear there.

Did not write your recovery words down? If you saved a wallet in this browser with a PIN or password, its encrypted copy is still stored in this browser. Do not clear this site’s browsing data until you have recovered it.
